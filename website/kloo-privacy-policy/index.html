Kloo / Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Kloo is provided by SYNTHETIC REALITY SAC under the OSYRYS brand (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Policy explains how information is handled when you play Kloo on Meta Quest or contact us about the game.

This Policy covers Kloo. It does not cover our other games, the general Syry website, or a platform’s independent use of information.

Current version: Kloo stores progress and preferences on your headset. Room-aware features use optional spatial access. Meta account checks and an online leaderboard are planned and are not enabled as of the effective date; Section 3 explains their intended data use.

Privacy and deletion requests ↓

1. Information Kloo processes

  • Local progress and preferences. Kloo saves crossword answers and progress, completion records, hint and reward state, achievements, book interactions, tutorial progress, audio and accessibility settings, and the name and appearance you choose for Grandma. These records are stored in the app’s local storage on your headset.
  • Tracking and input. Headset position and orientation, controller input, and hand-tracking information are processed to display the experience and let you interact with it. Kloo does not make tracking recordings for OSYRYS.
  • Room and depth information. With the relevant system permission, Kloo uses the saved room information supplied by Meta, such as room surfaces and spatial layout, to place virtual objects. It also uses environmental depth to make objects appear correctly behind real-world surfaces. These features process information on the headset; Kloo does not upload room maps or depth data to OSYRYS. Passthrough imagery is displayed by the headset runtime. Kloo does not record it or access raw camera images.
  • Local technical information. A graphics cache stores information such as device model, app and graphics versions, rendering settings, a cache timestamp, and scene names to prepare and manage rendering. This cache is stored on the headset; Kloo does not upload it to OSYRYS.
  • Support information. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a description of a problem, screenshots, or diagnostic logs. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

The current game does not require a separate OSYRYS account and does not include voice chat, microphone recording, advertising, or an OSYRYS gameplay-analytics service. Meta and other platform providers may process technical information independently, as described in Section 4.

2. Why information is used

Kloo uses local information to run the game, remember your progress and choices, provide rewards and accessible interactions, place mixed-reality content, and manage rendering. We use information you send to support to answer your request, investigate problems, and address privacy or security concerns.

We do not sell Kloo player information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Planned Meta online features

Meta account checks and the leaderboard are not enabled in the current version. We plan to use Meta’s platform services for the following limited purposes:

  • Account identity and access: an app-specific Meta user ID will identify the same player across sessions, and an entitlement result will confirm whether the account has access to Kloo.
  • Leaderboard: the player’s Meta username, the number of distinct crosswords completed, and leaderboard rank will allow players to recognize their entry and compare progress. The completion total will be submitted to Meta’s leaderboard service and associated with the player’s app-specific identity.
  • Visibility: usernames, completion totals, and ranks will be visible to other players viewing the leaderboard. Local crossword answers, room information, tracking data, and Grandma’s chosen name will not be submitted as part of the leaderboard.

These planned features do not access Meta identity or submit leaderboard records while they are disabled. We will update this Policy before enabling them to reflect the released behavior, retention, and deletion controls. We will also provide notice of public leaderboard participation before scores are shared.

4. Providers and sharing

Meta provides the headset operating system, account, store, permissions, and device services, and is the intended provider of the online features described above. Meta may independently process device, account, usage, diagnostic, or purchase information under the Meta Privacy Policy. OSYRYS does not receive your Meta password or full payment-card details through Kloo.

Kloo is built with Unity and Meta SDKs. Kloo’s Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and Unity Cloud Diagnostics reporting are disabled. Meta and other platform or SDK services may independently process diagnostic information under their own privacy notices, including the Unity Privacy Policy.

Our email provider processes messages sent to support. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information such as your IP address when you visit it; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We share information we control only as needed to handle your request, with service providers helping us operate the relevant service, or when required by applicable law or valid legal process. Information that stays only in Kloo’s local storage is not available to us remotely.

5. Retention

Local progress and preferences remain on your headset until they are overwritten or the app’s local data is cleared. Technical caches may be replaced as the app runs. Kloo does not keep a history of your real-time tracking or room depth for OSYRYS.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve the request and handle related follow-up or a specific legal obligation. We delete information we control when it is no longer needed or when you request deletion, unless applicable law requires retention; we will explain any such exception.

Meta, GitHub, and other providers may retain information they process independently under their own privacy notices. Planned leaderboard records are not being collected by the current version.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

Anyone, in any region, can request access, correction, or deletion of Kloo information we control, free of charge. Email hello@syry.io with the subject “Kloo privacy request.” Explain your request and provide enough information to locate the relevant support conversation or, once online features are available, the relevant player record. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request is yours. Do not send passwords.

  • Delete local data: use your headset’s app-storage controls to clear Kloo’s app data. This removes saved progress and preferences. Contact us if you need help locating the appropriate device controls. Resetting an individual crossword is not a full-data deletion and may retain completion records and earned rewards.
  • Control room access: you can decline or revoke Kloo’s spatial-data permission in Meta Quest’s app-permission settings. Room-aware features may be unavailable, but core crossword play remains available. Clearing Kloo’s data does not delete the room setup maintained separately by Meta; manage that through the headset’s system settings.
  • Platform information: Meta account and platform records are managed through Meta. We cannot directly delete information held independently by another provider, but can help identify the relevant provider.

Depending on the law where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of information, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a data-protection authority. You can contact us at the address above to exercise applicable rights.

7. Players aged 13 and older

Kloo is intended for players aged 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact hello@syry.io so we can investigate and delete information we control without undue delay.

If you are under the age of legal majority where you live, ask a parent or legal guardian to review this Policy with you. Meta’s family and parental-supervision tools may provide additional controls.

8. Security and policy changes

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information we control. No device or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. You can report a privacy or security concern to hello@syry.io.

We will keep this page up to date as Kloo changes, revise the effective date, and provide an in-app or other appropriate notice before a material change to our data practices takes effect. New online, diagnostic, or social features will be described before we use them to process additional personal information.
